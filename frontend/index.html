How Smart City 360 Works

A transparent, 4-step pipeline that turns public complaints into accountable government action.

1

Report with Map & Photo

Citizens submit issues with exact GPS coordinates, photos, and severity. Real-time duplicate checking prevents noise.

2

Community Prioritisation

Our intelligent multi-factor algorithm scores areas based on frequency, safety risks, and verified community confirmations.

3

Official Department Routing

Complaints route immediately to designated engineers across 9 municipal departments with SLA timeframes.

4

Live Tracking & Resolution

Citizens track status progress, view resolution evidence photos, or reopen tickets if the problem remains unaddressed.
