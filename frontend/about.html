MISSION & TRANSPARENCY

Your Voice. Our City. A Better Tomorrow.

Smart City 360 is an intelligent civic issue reporting and resolution system that bridges citizens and local government authorities through interactive maps and community-driven prioritisation.

1. Project Objectives

The primary objective of Smart City 360 is to make civic complaint reporting simple, transparent, and accessible to every citizen. Traditional grievance portals often function as black holes where tickets linger for months without updates. Smart City 360 solves this through:

  • Geographic Transparency: Pinpointing civic issues on Leaflet + OpenStreetMap maps to reveal high-density crisis clusters.
  • Intelligent Community Prioritization: Automatically calculating priority scores using frequency, unresolved backlog, safety severity, recency, and citizen confirmations.
  • Closed-Loop Accountability: Full audit history tracking each status transition, accompanied by official engineer remarks and photo evidence of resolution.
  • Duplicate Issue Mitigation: Suggesting existing nearby issues to citizens so they can upvote rather than cluttering municipal queues.

2. Citizen Privacy Policy

Smart City 360 adheres strictly to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act principles:

Data Masking:

Citizen phone numbers and email addresses are NEVER displayed in public feeds or community reports. Phone numbers are securely masked (e.g. +91 984*****77).

Location Safety:

Location coordinates are captured solely to identify the public infrastructure needing repair. Private home boundaries are not harvested.

Access Control:

Only authorized and authenticated municipal officials have permission to inspect full contact numbers for direct field verification.

3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does the priority algorithm determine what gets fixed first?

A: The system calculates a score from 0 to 100 based on: Report Frequency (30%), Unresolved Backlog (25%), Inherent Danger / Severity (20%), Recency (15%), and Community Support Upvotes (10%). Issues with a score >= 80 are flagged as Critical.

Q: What if the authority marks my complaint resolved, but the problem still exists?

A: Citizens can open the "Track Complaint" page and click "Reopen Complaint Ticket". This automatically increases the issue's priority score and alerts higher-level zonal officers.

Q: Do I need to create an account to report an issue?

A: No! Any citizen can submit a complaint immediately by providing their name and mobile number. Creating an account simply allows you to manage all your submitted complaints from a personal dashboard.
