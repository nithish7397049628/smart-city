MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION

The Smart City 360 Priority Formula

80–100: Critical 60–79: High 35–59: Medium 0–34: Low
Priority Score = (Report Frequency × 30%) + (Unresolved Complaints × 25%) + (Severity × 20%) + (Recency × 15%) + (Unique Reporters × 10%)
1. Frequency (30%)
Density of citizen complaints clustered within the same 500m radius.
2. Unresolved (25%)
Long-standing complaints that have surpassed target SLA resolution days.
3. Severity (20%)
Inherent hazard level (e.g. snapped 440V cables vs broken park bench).
4. Recency (15%)
Higher weighting given to issues emerging rapidly in the past 24–72 hours.
5. Citizen Upvotes (10%)
Independent public verification votes submitted by neighbourhood residents.
